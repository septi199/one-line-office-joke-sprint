# Notes — One-Line Office Joke: 24-Hour Sprint

## The line

"Our team building exercise was a trust fall. Nobody caught anyone, but the spreadsheet documenting it has 14 tabs now." (118 chars)

## Why this line

- One line about office life: a trust-fall team building exercise, a setup anyone who has sat in an office recognises.
- The punchline is specific, not generic: the failure wasn't the fall, it was the 14-tab spreadsheet documenting it. Bureaucracy as the real product of the exercise.
- Single sentence flow, no explanation needed, tight wording.

## Program

`main.ts` writes the line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`) and logs the character count. Deterministic: identical bytes on every run.
